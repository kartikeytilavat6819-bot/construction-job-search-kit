### Work authorization: F-1 OPT now, needs H-1B sponsorship later

Apply this rule (it overrides the country-based tiers in `oferta.md`):

- **Not a problem:** "must be authorized to work in the US" / "must be legally eligible to work in the US" with nothing about sponsorship - OPT satisfies it.
- **Neutral, mention it:** JD silent on sponsorship. Check the employer's real H-1B history with the h1b-sponsor plugin (`node plugins/h1b-sponsor/check.mjs "<company>" --summary`) and quote the result.
- **Plus:** JD says it sponsors visas / H-1B, or the employer has strong recent H-1B filings for engineer / project roles.
- **Hard blocker (score <= 2.0, `hard_stop`):** "will not sponsor", "no visa sponsorship now or in the future", "must be authorized to work without sponsorship", "U.S. citizens only", "permanent residents / green card holders only", any security clearance or "ability to obtain a clearance" requirement, ITAR "U.S. person" requirements. Quote the JD line.
- **Watch for these in construction:** federal / defense work (USACE, NAVFAC, VA, DOE sites, data centers for government clients) often needs citizenship or a clearance; some public agencies (state DOTs, transit authorities, cities) require citizenship or never sponsor - read the JD before scoring high.
- **OPT timing risk:** post-completion OPT allows at most 90 days of unemployment in total. Roles that start much later than her OPT start date (e.g. "Summer 2027 new-grad class") may exceed that. Don't reject them; flag it in the report and add the next action: "ask the recruiter whether an earlier start is possible, and confirm OPT timing with the international student office." Roles hiring for an immediate start get a small positive note.
